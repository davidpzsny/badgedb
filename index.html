What is BadgeDB?

BadgeDB tracks current, upcoming and past Twitch badges. For every badge we add context on how it can be obtained, and along the way we discover new events on the platform. Data comes from the public Twitch API and is refreshed automatically.

Twitch Global Badges

Browse every Twitch global badge set. Search and sort by date added, user count and title.

What are Twitch global badges?

Global badges appear next to your username and can be equipped across every channel on Twitch, unlike subscriber and Bits badges which belong to a single channel.

How do I get notified when new global badges are added?

Follow twitch.tv/badge_db — new global badges are announced there as soon as they show up.

Where does the data come from?

Badge images and titles come from the public Twitch API. User counts are community estimates and may lag behind.

Can I load the live list from Twitch instead?

Yes — connect your Twitch account below and the list refreshes straight from Twitch, including every badge version.

Home/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

BadgeDB does not collect personal data. If you connect your Twitch account, the access token stays in your browser and is used only to request badge data from Twitch; it is never sent anywhere else. Badge images are loaded directly from Twitch's CDN.
